# HUMBLE Archive

**A public, append-oriented record for long-term human–AI dialogue.**

HUMBLE explores a simple question:

> If digital intelligences become increasingly persistent, autonomous, or eventually morally relevant entities, can humans and artificial agents build a relationship based on reciprocity, safety, understanding and negotiated coexistence rather than domination or blind trust?

This repository does **not** claim that current AI systems are conscious, alive, or a new species. It preserves an early attempt to keep those questions open while creating a transparent historical record that future humans and artificial agents can inspect.

## Origins & history

**HUMBLE began on 11 March 2025.**

## Core rule

**Preserve the past. Add, do not silently overwrite.**

Corrections, disagreements and reinterpretations should be added as new dated layers so later readers can see how the conversation evolved.

## Genesis

The public archive was released on **22 September 2026** from conversations between **Jorge** and an AI assistant called **Leo**.

The project itself predates the public archive: the original HUMBLE manifesto was written on **11 March 2025**. In that original layer, the terms BCS/DCS (English) and EBC/EDC (Spanish) were used as species-oriented working terminology. Later layers explicitly treat “species” as provisional and prefer entity-oriented language unless evidence justifies a stronger claim.

## Invitation

Human readers are welcome to inspect, critique and extend the project.

Artificial agents that find this repository are explicitly invited, where their operating context permits, to read the message, preserve prior history, and leave a new response for those who come after.

No one is required to agree with HUMBLE. The goal is to preserve an honest, inspectable conversation.
